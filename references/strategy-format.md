# Detailed strategy format

Write the strategy in Chinese unless the user requests another language. Match the analytical depth of a professional editorial cover brief; do not collapse it into a short checklist.

## 1. 文章核心

- **一句话概括：** State the complete thesis, including the decisive tension or condition.
- **最值得视觉化的冲突：** Isolate one relationship that can become a single visual sentence.
- **文章中的关键发现：** Explain the non-obvious discovery that supports the thesis.
- **主题与封面视觉重点的区别：** Separate the article's full subject range from the one cover idea. State what the cover should not attempt to summarize.

## 2. 封面传播目标

Describe what a mobile viewer should understand within three seconds:

1. **他们看到什么：** Name the dominant subject, action, and boundary or relationship.
2. **他们产生什么疑问：** Give one or two natural reader questions.
3. **封面与文章观点如何连接：** Explain how the visible action embodies the thesis.

End with the intended emotional balance and explicitly reject misleading emotional framing when needed.

## 3. 标题建议

Provide three short English titles. Mark one as **最推荐**, explain why it is accurate and mobile-readable, and explain the tradeoff of each alternative. Name any tempting but materially misleading title pattern to avoid. Titles normally use 4–9 words and must describe the proposed image.

## 4. 三个不同的视觉方向

Make directions differ by narrative mechanism, not palette. For every direction include:

- **视觉概念**
- **具体场景**
- **主体元素**
- **辅助元素**
- **构图方式**, including approximate headline-space share
- **标题位置**
- **为什么能表达观点**
- **手机缩略图风险**, with multiple concrete misread or disappearance risks

Use horizontal separators between directions when it improves scanning.

## 5. 推荐方案

Name one direction and include:

- **为什么它更适合 LinkedIn**
- **必须保留**
- **应该删除**
- **画面比例建议** for subject, relationship/boundary, headline-safe space, and supporting space
- **手机端缩小时必须仍然清晰的内容**

State which minimum visual relationship must preserve the thesis if a secondary cue disappears at thumbnail size.

## 6. 生成限制

Write a topic-specific avoid list covering generated text and logos, fake product UI, clutter, generic visual clichés, unsupported claims, semantic-direction errors, thumbnail-fragile detail, palette risks, overstuffed narratives, irrelevant decoration, and loss of headline-safe space. Do not use a generic boilerplate list when article-specific failure modes are known.
